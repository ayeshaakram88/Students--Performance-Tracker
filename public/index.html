About Us

Student Performance Tracker is a web-based application developed using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, NodeJS and Oracle Database. This application provides an easy way for student in searching the details of their academic attendance and marks details. Students and parents can search the academic performence and the details of attendance and marks percentages, added by admin and the teacher.
